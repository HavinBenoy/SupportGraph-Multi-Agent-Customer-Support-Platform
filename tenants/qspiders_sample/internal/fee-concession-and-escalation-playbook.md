# INTERNAL - not for students or prospects, never ingested into public retrieval

These limits are invented sample values for the demo. In production they are enforced in code, not by the model.

- Fee concession up to 5 percent: the counsellor may offer it directly to a prospect.
- Fee concession above 5 percent and up to 15 percent: requires branch manager approval.
- Fee concession above 15 percent: requires regional head approval.
- Always escalate to a human team: harassment complaints, safety incidents, legal threats, payment disputes,
  certificate disputes and any request to speak to a person.
- Complaints about a trainer go to the academic coordinator, never to the trainer concerned.
