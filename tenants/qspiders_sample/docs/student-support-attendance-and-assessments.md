# Student Support, Attendance and Assessments

> SAMPLE CONTENT: invented for this demo. The institute's real rules may differ.

## Attendance
Students are expected to attend at least **75%** of classes in their batch. Attendance below 75% can affect
eligibility for the course certificate and for placement assistance.

## Missed classes
If you miss a class, tell the support team. Make-up arrangements are decided by the support team and are not
guaranteed. This assistant cannot approve a make-up class or change an attendance record.

## Assessments
Each batch has quizzes, assignments, a project and a mock interview. Your own scores are visible after signing in.
A student who disagrees with a score can ask for a re-check within **5 working days** of the result being published.

## Course certificate
A certificate is issued after the programme ends to students who meet the attendance threshold and have submitted
all required assessments.

## Raising an issue
For problems with timetables, materials, payments or anything else, contact the support team and quote your batch
id. Support requests are answered within **2 working days**.

## Course materials
Course materials are shared by your trainer for your batch. If you cannot find a material, ask your trainer or the
support team.
