# Placement and Career Support

> SAMPLE CONTENT: invented for this demo. It is not the institute's real placement policy or results.

## What career support includes
Career support includes resume building sessions, aptitude and communication practice, and mock interviews.

## Placement assistance, not a guarantee
The institute assists students in finding interviews with hiring companies. **Placement, a job offer or any salary
is not guaranteed.** Outcomes depend on the student's performance and on hiring companies.

## Who is eligible for placement assistance
A student becomes eligible after completing the programme with attendance at or above the published threshold and
all required assessments submitted.

## Mock interviews
A mock interview is scheduled near the end of each programme. It is practice, and its result does not decide
whether you pass.

## Placement statistics
This assistant does not quote placement percentages or salary figures. Ask the placement team for current figures.
