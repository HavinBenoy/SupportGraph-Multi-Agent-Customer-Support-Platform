# Privacy and Student Data

> SAMPLE CONTENT: invented for this demo. It is not the institute's real privacy policy.

## Who can see your records
A signed-in student can see only their own attendance, schedule and scores. This assistant cannot show one
student's records to another student, and it will decline such requests.

## Questions from parents or friends
This assistant does not discuss a student's records with other people. The student can sign in and share the
information themselves.

## What this assistant cannot do
This assistant explains information and reads your own records. It cannot change attendance, change marks,
enrol or withdraw anyone, or promise a refund or concession.

## Personal details
Do not type passwords or payment card numbers into this chat. The assistant never needs them.
