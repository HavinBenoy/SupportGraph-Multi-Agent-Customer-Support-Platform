# Eligibility and Prerequisites

> SAMPLE CONTENT: invented for this demo. The institute's real eligibility rules may differ.

## Who can join
Graduates, final-year students and working professionals who want to change career can join. A degree in
computer science is not required.

## Programme prerequisites
- Python Full Stack: no prior coding experience needed.
- Data Science with Python: basic school-level mathematics and a willingness to learn Python.
- Software Testing: no prior experience needed; basic computer skills are enough.

## Documents needed at admission
A government-issued photo ID and your latest academic marks card are needed when you register.

## Final-year students
Final-year students can join a batch while studying, provided class timings do not clash with their college
schedule. The student is responsible for checking this before enrolling.
