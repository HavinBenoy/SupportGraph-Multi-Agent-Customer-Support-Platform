# Fees and Payments

> SAMPLE CONTENT: every amount below is invented for this demo and is not the institute's real fee.

## Programme fees
| Programme | Fee (INR) |
|---|---|
| Python Full Stack | 45,000 |
| Data Science with Python | 55,000 |
| Software Testing | 35,000 |

## Paying in instalments
The fee can be paid in two instalments. The first instalment is **60%** of the fee, paid at registration. The
second instalment of **40%** is paid before week 8 of the batch.

## Payment methods
You can pay by UPI, debit or credit card, or net banking. A receipt is emailed after each payment.

## Fee refunds
A refund request made within **7 days of the first class** is reviewed by the accounts team. Requests after that
are generally not accepted. Submitting a request does not guarantee a refund.

## Fee concessions
This help assistant cannot offer or promise discounts or concessions. Please ask the admissions team.
