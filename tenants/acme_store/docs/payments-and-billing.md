# Payments and Billing

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal.

## When you are charged
Your card is authorised when you place the order and charged when the order ships. A pending authorisation may
appear on your statement before then; it drops off automatically if the order is cancelled.

## Promo codes
One promo code can be used per order. Codes cannot be applied after an order has been placed.

## Charge questions
If you see a charge you do not recognise or believe you were charged twice, contact support with your order number.
A member of our team reviews billing disputes.
