# Shipping and Delivery

## Where we ship
We currently ship **within the United States only**. We do not offer international shipping at this time.

## Delivery options
| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 business days | $4.99 (free on orders over $50) |
| Express | 1-2 business days | $14.99 |

Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern on a business day usually ship the same day. Orders placed later ship the next
business day. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US public holidays.

## Tracking
When your order ships we email a tracking number. We ship with UPS, FedEx and DHL. You can also see the carrier and
tracking number by asking our support assistant about your order.

## Late or missing packages
If your package has not arrived **7 days after its estimated delivery date**, contact us and we will open a carrier
investigation. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we send a replacement or refund you in full.

## Delivered but not received
Check with neighbours and around your property first. If you still cannot find it within 48 hours of the delivered
status, contact us.
