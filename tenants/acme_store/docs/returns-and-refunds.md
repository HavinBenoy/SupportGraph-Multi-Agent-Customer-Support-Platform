# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
You can return most items within **30 days of delivery**. The 30 days start on the day your order is marked
delivered, not the day you placed it. An order that has not been delivered yet cannot be returned; if you no
longer want it, see our cancellation policy.

## Condition
Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all accessories included. We may refuse a return that
shows signs of use or damage that was not present on arrival.

## What cannot be returned
- Gift cards
- Opened software or digital downloads
- Personalised or engraved items
- Items marked "Final sale" on the product page

## Return shipping
Returns are free if the item arrived defective, damaged, or was not what you ordered. For any other reason, a
**$6.95 return shipping fee** is deducted from your refund. We email you a prepaid return label once your return
is approved.

## Refunds
Once we receive and inspect your return, we refund the original payment method within **5-7 business days**.
Your bank may take extra time to show the credit. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return
is due to our error. If an item is partly damaged or incomplete, we may issue a partial refund.

## Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order.
