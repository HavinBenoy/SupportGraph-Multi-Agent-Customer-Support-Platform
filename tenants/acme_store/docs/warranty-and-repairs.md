# Warranty and Repairs

## What is covered
Electronics sold by Acme Store carry a **12-month limited warranty** from the delivery date. It covers defects in
materials and workmanship that appear during normal use.

## What is not covered
Accidental damage, liquid damage, wear and tear, unauthorised repairs, and damage from using the product other
than as intended.

## Making a claim
Contact support with your order number and a short description and photos of the problem. If the claim is
accepted we repair or replace the item at our discretion. We do not refund warranty claims in cash unless a
replacement is unavailable.

## Within the return window
If a product is faulty within the first 30 days, you can choose to return it for a refund instead of using the
warranty.
