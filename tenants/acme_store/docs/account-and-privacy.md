# Your Account and Privacy

## Resetting your password
Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your email. We send a reset link that is valid for one hour.
Support cannot see or set your password.

## Staying safe
We will **never** ask for your password, a full card number, or a verification code by email, chat or phone.
If someone does, do not reply and tell us.

## Changing your email address
For security, support cannot change the email on an account from chat alone. Sign in and update it under Account
settings. If you cannot sign in, contact support and we will verify your identity first.

## Your data
We only share order details with the person who owns the order. You can request a copy or deletion of your personal
data by emailing privacy@acme.example; we respond within 30 days.
