# INTERNAL - not for customers, never ingested into customer-facing retrieval

These thresholds are enforced in code (guardrails), not by the model. They are listed here for the humans on the team.

- Refund up to $50: agent may propose; no human needed beyond the standard approval step.
- Refund over $50 and up to $500: requires human approval.
- Refund over $500: requires supervisor approval.
- Always escalate to a human: legal threats, safety incidents (fire, injury), suspected account takeover,
  billing disputes, and any request to speak to a person.
